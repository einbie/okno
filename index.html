Okno

Tap your free windows — the message writes itself.

Someone asks when you are free. Okno reads the calendar already on your iPhone, you tap the windows that suit you, and the message is on your clipboard — ready to paste anywhere.

Everything stays on your phone

No servers, no accounts, no analytics, no ads. Okno makes no network requests at all. It does not even show what your meetings are called — busy time is simply “Busy”.

Coming to the App Store. English and Russian, free, no purchases of any kind.
